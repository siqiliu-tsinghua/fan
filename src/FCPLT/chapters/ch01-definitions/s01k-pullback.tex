\paragraph{\trPullback。}\label{sub:pullback}
任何\trNonunital{} \trAFunctor{} $\scr{G}\colon\scr{A}\to\scr{B}$ 都诱导一个%
\textbf{\trPullback\trFunctor}（pullback functor）
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pullback-functor-definition}
\scr{G}^*=\scr{R}_{\scr{G}^{\mathrm{opp}}}\colon
\mathit{nu}\text{-}\mathit{mod}(\scr{B})
\longrightarrow\mathit{nu}\text{-}\mathit{mod}(\scr{A}).
\end{equation}
正如任何右\trComposition{}一样，它的高阶项全部消没，因而是 dg \trCategory{}之间的一个%
\trNonunital{} dg \trFunctor。具体地，
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
(\scr{G}^*\scr{M})(X)&=\scr{M}(\scr{G}(X)),\\
\mu^d_{\scr{G}*\scr{M}}(b,a_{d-1},\ldots,a_1)
&=\sum_r\sum_{s_1,\ldots,s_r}\mu^r_{\scr{M}}
\bigl(b,\scr{G}^{s_r}(a_{d-1},\ldots,a_{d-s_r}),\\
&\qquad\qquad\ldots,\scr{G}^{s_1}(a_{s_1},\ldots,a_1)\bigr),\\
(\scr{G}^*t)^d(b,a_{d-1},\ldots,a_1)
&=\sum_r\sum_{s_1,\ldots,s_r}t^r
\bigl(b,\scr{G}^{s_r}(a_{d-1},\ldots,a_{d-s_r}),\\
&\qquad\qquad\ldots,\scr{G}^{s_1}(a_{s_1},\ldots,a_1)\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
